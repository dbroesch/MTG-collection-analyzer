Collection Analyzer

Track your Magic: The Gathering collection, discover missing cards, and monitor your investment

📖 How to Use

1

Create Your Collection

Sign up at Archidekt and add your MTG cards to your collection. You can search and add cards manually or import from other sources.

2

Export as CSV

Go to your Collection page on Archidekt, click the export/download button, and select CSV format. Save the file to your computer.

3

Upload & Analyze

Drag your CSV file into the upload area below (or click to browse). We'll compare your collection against Scryfall's database to find missing cards.

4

Export Missing Cards

Copy or download your missing card lists in Card Kingdom or Star City Games format to easily purchase what you need.
